# Sandstone licence

> **Draft.** This explains in plain words how Sandstone may be used. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before you sell the paid plans.

Sandstone comes in three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Team**. Plans and prices are on the [pricing page](https://lkb00.github.io/sandstone/#pricing).

## Free

The free core covers:
- the documentation, patterns, case studies and guides
- the design tokens
- the parts marked **Free** on the site: the basic building blocks and the parts of a plain chat

You may use the free core in any project, personal or commercial, and change it as you like. Keep this licence notice with any copy of the source you share.

## Pro and Team (paid)

A paid licence covers:
- every part, marked **Pro** on the site
- the page templates and whole screens
- the AI-agent kit: the MCP server, `components.json` and the Markdown page for each part

**Who may use it**
- **Pro:** one person.
- **Team:** up to 10 people at one company.

**What you may do**
- Use the parts in unlimited projects, including work for clients and products you sell.
- Change the parts to fit your product.

**What you may not do**
- Resell, share or give away the parts, the templates or the AI-agent kit, whether on their own or as part of another UI kit, design system, template or theme.
- Share your licence key or the download with people it does not cover.

**Updates**
- You get updates for one year from the date you buy.
- Everything you received in that year is yours to keep using after that.

**Payment and refunds**
- Lemon Squeezy, the seller of record, handles payment, receipts and sales tax or VAT.
- Refunds follow the policy shown at checkout.
